# Code of conduct

This page applies to everyone who takes part in the Atalanta project: in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space.

Atalanta follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Its full text is the standard for this project. In short:

- Be respectful. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- Assume good intent, and be clear and specific when you give feedback.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Do not publish anyone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers at <atalanta@atomicmail.io>. Say what happened, where, and include links or screenshots if you have them. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainers review every report. They may remove or edit comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may block a person from the project temporarily or permanently.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for those.
